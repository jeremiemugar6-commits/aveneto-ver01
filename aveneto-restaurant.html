Our Story

Ameritalian
Since 1983

AVeneto is more than a restaurant — it's where the bold spirit of America meets the soulful tradition of Italy. Born in 1983, we've been crafting pasta and pizza that feels like home for over four decades.

Our kitchen stays true to the classics: slow-cooked marinara, hand-stretched dough, generous portions, and recipes that have never changed — because some things are perfect just the way they are.
